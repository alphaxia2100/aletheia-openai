This is an evolving ledger. “Promising” is not “validated.” Each mechanism belongs on its own branch
unless a dependency makes isolation impossible.

## Newly observed pitfalls in the self-survey

1. The keyword router classified a meta-research branch as a consumer-product question.
2. Root anchoring polluted exact named-method searches.
3. Candidates with zero subject hits were admitted by citation/weak lexical signals.
4. Discovery channel class was confused with document epistemic role.
5. Anti-bot, scanned-image, paywall, and wrong-page bodies passed the length-only read-success gate.
6. Manual primary recovery was necessary but incompletely integrated into runtime telemetry.
7. Brave was degraded and GitHub unauthenticated; the survey must not claim full channel coverage.

These are direct operational observations, not prevalence estimates. They remain in the final gaps
until a separate mechanism fixes and forward-tests them.
