# Decision log

This log preserves decisions and their evidence so a later audit cannot silently
turn a diagnostic into a product claim.

| When | Decision | Rationale and evidence | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Before the first live request (author assertion) | Separate source access from full workflow. | The earlier controlled comparison froze the source packet, so its corrected 11–11 factual tie could not answer the access question. | The primary study is a small model-free retrieval/candidate-replay comparison, not an answer-quality bakeoff. |
| At independent review | Do not certify preregistration timing. | `tasks.json`, `protocol.md`, and the runner are untracked. Their hashes match the observation, but no committed baseline, signed tag, or external timestamp proves that they were frozen before the run. | Call this a claimed preregistered operational pilot; future studies must commit and pin the full harness before retrieval. |
| Before execution | Pin the runtime and isolate mutable configuration/cache. | The observation records a clean detached runtime at `5bea63a9ffb13e9de0e94f8ba38c426a301faaf8`; the user's checkout contains unrelated dirty runtime/skill/test work. | This audit changes only its own folder and excludes the dirty checkout from executed connector/ranker code. |
| First live observation | Preserve the literal primary score rather than massage it. | Generic was 1/3 and specialist 0/3 under the frozen URL-substring rule; raw result hash `sha256:8157f3e636e5400af80edcde9addf2a245fd4621805a6cc9190c3b3c49804db8`. | The literal score remains visible, with no retroactive reclassification. |
| After reviewing primary behavior | Do not retroactively repair the PubMed matcher. | Europe PMC returns the intended work under a PMC URL and the adapter does not retain PMID as typed identity. Changing the task/matcher after output would invalidate the observed endpoint. | Mark biomedical semantic score `NE`; retain the literal `false`; design a typed-ID follow-up. |
| After reviewing primary behavior | Run one separate replication and an explicit post-hoc adapter diagnostic. | The question was whether a miss represented absent access or broken query/adapter behavior. | Outputs are separately named and excluded from the primary denominator; their hashes are retained in [results.json](results.json). |
| After replication | Treat live ranking as unstable, not as a win/loss reversal. | The generic autoresearch result disappeared minutes later; specialist literal misses persisted and OpenAlex again returned two HTTP 400s. | Require interleaved repeated trials and immutable receipts in any promotion study. |
| After diagnostics | Attribute errors to the operational path, not source nonexistence. | Exact identifier/slug calls find Europe PMC, arXiv, and GitHub targets; OpenAlex fails specifically on terminal `?`; natural GitHub compaction returns zero. | Current finding is “unreliable conversion of public access into discovery,” not “specialist sources are useless.” |
| After integrity review | Do not call replay independently reproducible from the saved raw artifact. | The saved projection omits fields consumed by the ranker, changing two nonempty specialist replay orders when regenerated. | Treat the in-memory autoresearch retention result as narrow; require a complete, content-addressed candidate ledger next time. |
| After final claims review | Preserve execution errata without rewriting the historical protocol. | The primary loops over three tasks: two adapters per task × access lane means six adapter invocations per aggregate lane and 12 overall, not two physical HTTP requests. Generic/specialist calls were sequential, and channel-local versus pooled ranks are not comparable. | [results.md](results.md) and the manifest state the corrected units/snapshot boundary; [protocol.md](protocol.md) remains untouched as the historical input. |
| Final audit ruling | Reject the present “much better because of source access” claim. | No specialist-only semantic discovery is established; public known-ID lookup is not unique access; full-text, answer quality, cost, and direct-equal-tools comparisons are absent. | Do not market or default to a complex source/multi-agent workflow on this basis. |
| Future change gate | Keep only evidence-backed access policies. | The v2 design requires fixed budgets, canonical identity receipts, a strong direct same-tools control, and blinded outcome scoring. | A failing policy is reverted/disabled by default, with its evidence retained for future diagnosis. |
